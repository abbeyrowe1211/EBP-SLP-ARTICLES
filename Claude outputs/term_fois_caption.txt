📚 TERM OF THE WEEK

FOIS — Functional Oral Intake Scale

If you work in dysphagia, you've charted this. The FOIS is a 7-point clinician-rated scale describing a patient's level of oral intake for nutrition and hydration — from fully tube-dependent (level 1) to a totally unrestricted oral diet (level 7). It was originally validated in acute stroke patients (Crary, Mann & Groher, 2005) and has since become one of the most widely used functional outcome measures in dysphagia research.

Why it's worth knowing cold:
🔹 It's a common outcome measure across dysphagia studies — useful for comparing your own patient's trajectory to the literature
🔹 It captures functional diet status even when instrumental findings (like a repeat MBSS) look similar visit to visit
🔹 It's a single number — fast to document, easy to trend over time, and easy to communicate to the team
