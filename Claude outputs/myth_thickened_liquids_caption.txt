🩺 MYTH VS. FACT

❌ MYTH: Thickened liquids eliminate aspiration risk.

✅ FACT: Thickened liquids can reduce aspiration frequency on instrumental exam — but they don't eliminate the risk. The dysphagia literature has raised real concerns about increased pharyngeal residue, dehydration, reduced quality of life, and inconsistent effects on actual pneumonia rates.

Clinical takeaway: diet modification is one tool in the toolbox, not a guarantee. Weigh it against hydration status, post-swallow residue, and the patient's own preferences and quality of life — and let instrumental assessment (not assumption) drive the recommendation.
