🗺️ ASSESSMENT PATHWAY: Suspected Acquired Stuttering

A quick visual for the intake-to-treatment flow when an adult presents with new-onset stuttering:

1. Onset & Referral — get the full timeline and context
2. Rule Out Organic Causes — concurrent neuro workup (stroke, TBI, medication, disease process)
3. Baseline Fluency Assessment — stuttered syllables across tasks, secondary behaviors, adaptation effect
4. Differential Diagnosis — neurogenic vs. functional/psychogenic vs. developmental
5. Structured Treatment — hierarchy-based intervention with outcome tracking
