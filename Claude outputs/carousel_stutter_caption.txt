🚩 5 RED FLAGS: Acquired vs. Developmental Stuttering

Adult presents with new-onset stuttering — how do you know it's not just "always been there and never got addressed"? Here are 5 differentiating features worth knowing cold, especially if it's tied to a neurological event or sudden stressor.

1️⃣ Sudden adult onset — no childhood history, often after a neurological event or major stressor
2️⃣ Any word can trip — disfluencies show up on function words and simple words too, not just content words
3️⃣ No task lets up — similar dysfluency rates across ALL tasks, including automatic speech
4️⃣ Few secondary behaviors — struggle, avoidance, and situational fear are typically minimal
5️⃣ No adaptation effect — little to no improvement across repeated readings of the same passage

Bottom line: rule out neurogenic and organic causes concurrently, then move toward a structured, hierarchy-based assessment and treatment plan.
