🎵 THIS WEEK'S ARTICLE, IN ACTION: Music-Based Interventions for Nonfluent Aphasia

This week's Article of the Week — Koshimori et al., 2025 (Annals of the NY Academy of Sciences) — is a systematic review of 10 RCTs on music-based interventions (MIT, Modified MIT, singing-based approaches) for nonfluent aphasia. MBIs showed real benefit for repetition and naming, even head-to-head with standard speech therapy — though functional communication outcomes were only measured in 2 of the 10 trials.

So what does that actually look like from referral to treatment? Here's the pathway, including exactly where EBP SLP has your back:

2️⃣ Screen for MIT Candidacy — preserved melodic/rhythm perception, and standard word-retrieval approaches have plateaued
3️⃣ Pull the Evidence (in the app) — search "Melodic Intonation Therapy" in your Article Library for the full MIT/Modified MIT protocol

The evidence base has real limitations (moderate-to-high risk of bias, small functional-communication sample), so pair MIT with outcome measures that actually capture real-world change.
