Privacy Policy

EBP SLP — Last updated: May 2026

Overview

EBP SLP ("the App") is a clinical reference tool designed for speech-language pathologists. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or contact information.
  • We do not collect any patient data or clinical notes.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.

Data Stored Locally

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Saved articles (bookmarks)
  • Session plans and caseload data you create
  • Your profile name (optional)

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Analytics

The App does not use any third-party analytics or tracking tools.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for licensed healthcare professionals and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
