EBP SLP

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EBP SLP, developed by Rowe to Recovery LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), handles information when you use our app. By using EBP SLP, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

EBP SLP may collect the following types of information automatically:

  • Device information (device type, operating system version, IP address)
  • App usage data (features accessed, time spent, interactions)
  • In-app activity (articles saved, filters applied, session plans generated)

We do not collect your name, email address, or other personal identifying information unless you contact us directly.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Improve the App's features, content, and reliability
  • Understand how the evidence library and session planning tools are being used
  • Diagnose bugs and maintain App stability

3. AI-Powered Features

EBP SLP includes an AI session plan generator. When you use this feature, your request (including selected article context) is sent to a secure server and processed by Anthropic's AI. No personally identifiable information is included in these requests. AI-generated content is for reference purposes only and should always be reviewed by a licensed clinician before clinical use.

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services for analytics and AI processing. These services receive only anonymized or non-identifying data. We do not sell or rent your data to any third party.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Data you save locally (such as bookmarked articles) is stored on your device and is not transmitted to us. To request deletion of any data associated with your use of EBP SLP, contact us at info@ebpslpapp.com.

6. Children's Privacy

EBP SLP is designed for licensed healthcare professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of internet transmission or digital storage is completely secure.

8. Opting Out

You may stop all data collection by uninstalling EBP SLP using your device's standard uninstall process.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. When we do, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
