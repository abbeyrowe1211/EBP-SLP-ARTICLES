Privacy Policy

How EBP‑SLP handles information

Effective August 29, 2026 App EBP‑SLP Publisher Rowe to Recovery LLC

  • EBP‑SLP doesn't have accounts, logins, or passwords — we never collect your name, email, or identity.
  • Everything you save, write, or track in the app (articles, notes, session data, preferences) stays on your device. We don't have a server that receives it.
  • The only information that leaves your device is what Apple and RevenueCat need to process and manage a subscription — and it's tied to an anonymous ID, not to you.
  • EBP‑SLP is a clinical reference and planning tool, not a medical record system. It is not HIPAA-compliant, and it should never be used to store information that identifies a patient.

01Who we are

EBP‑SLP is a mobile application for medical speech-language pathologists, published by Rowe to Recovery LLC (“we,” “us,” “our”). This policy explains what information EBP‑SLP (the “App”) collects, what it doesn't, and how the small amount of information that does exist is handled.

This policy applies to the App itself. It doesn't apply to third-party sites you might reach by tapping a link inside the App, such as a source article's original journal page.

02What we don't collect

Because EBP‑SLP has no account system, there is no sign-up, no password, and no login of any kind. As a direct result, we never collect or have access to:

  • Your name, email address, or contact information
  • Your location
  • Contacts, photos, or other device content not entered directly into the App
  • Browsing activity outside the App
  • Advertising identifiers — EBP‑SLP contains no advertising or ad-tracking software

The App also does not include analytics or crash-reporting software of any kind. We don't know how you use the App, how often you open it, or whether it has crashed on your device.

03What's stored on your device

EBP‑SLP stores information locally on your device only, using standard iOS on-device app storage. None of the following is transmitted to us, to a server we operate, or to any third party:

Stored locallyWhat it is
Clinician profileThe name, credentials, and practice setting you optionally enter about yourself — never a patient.
Saved articles & groupsArticles you've saved to your library and how you've organized them into groups.
Article notesFreeform notes you write about an article, subject to the no-PHI rule described in Section 5.
Session & trial dataTrial-by-trial data you record during live data collection, subject to the same no-PHI rule.
In-progress draftsA temporary autosave of an active data-collection session, kept so an interrupted session can be recovered after an app restart.
Notification preferencesHow often, and on what day and time, you'd like reminder notifications.
Setup statusWhether you've completed onboarding and acknowledged the PHI notice.

Reminder notifications are scheduled directly on your device. EBP‑SLP does not use push notifications sent from a server, so no device push token is collected.

If you delete the App, all of the information above is permanently deleted along with it. We don't keep a copy, because we never had one.

04Subscriptions & payment

EBP‑SLP offers an optional paid subscription, processed entirely through Apple's In-App Purchase system. We never see or handle your card number, billing address, or other payment details — Apple processes those directly.

To activate and manage your subscription, the App uses RevenueCat, a subscription-infrastructure service. When you open the App, RevenueCat assigns your install an anonymous, randomly generated identifier — not your name or email — and receives the purchase, renewal, and entitlement information needed to know whether your subscription is active. This is the one piece of information that leaves your device in the normal course of using the App.

Apple's and RevenueCat's own privacy policies govern their handling of this data: Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

05Patient information & HIPAA

⚠ EBP-SLP is not HIPAA-compliant software

EBP‑SLP is a clinical reference and session-planning tool — not a medical record system or covered-entity software. It does not meet HIPAA's technical, administrative, or physical safeguard requirements, and it must never be used to store Protected Health Information (PHI).

Never enter any of the following into the App — in a note, a session title, a plan name, or anywhere else:

  • A patient's full name
  • Dates of birth or ages tied to a specific patient
  • Medical record numbers
  • Diagnosis codes or details tied to an identifiable individual
  • Any other detail that could identify a specific patient

If PHI is entered despite this, it is stored locally on your device only, in the same way as any other note — we do not receive it, review it, or have any way to access it. Responsibility for keeping PHI out of the App rests with you as the clinician using it. Use a treatment approach, a case code, or a general label when naming plans or sessions, and keep any link between that label and a real patient in your organization's own secure system.

06How information is used

Because nearly everything you enter stays on your device, there is very little for us to “use.” The device-local information described in Section 3 is used only by the App itself, on your device, to provide its features to you — showing your saved articles, remembering your notification preferences, and so on.

The anonymous subscription data described in Section 4 is used solely to verify and manage your subscription — to determine whether the App should unlock premium features for your install.

We do not use any information to build advertising profiles, to train other products, or for any purpose beyond operating the App and its subscription.

07Sharing & third parties

We do not sell information. We do not share information with data brokers or advertisers, because we don't collect information to share with them in the first place.

The only third parties involved in operating EBP‑SLP are:

  • Apple — distributes the App and processes all in-app purchase payments.
  • RevenueCat — manages subscription status using the anonymous identifier described in Section 4.

We may disclose information if required to by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Rowe to Recovery LLC, our users, or the public — though given how little we hold, there is very little to disclose.

08Retention & deletion

On-device information (Section 3) is retained on your device for as long as the App is installed, and is permanently deleted the moment you delete the App. There is no “delete my account” request to make with us, because there is no account and no copy of your data outside your device.

Subscription and transaction records (Section 4) are retained by Apple and RevenueCat according to their own retention practices — typically for as long as needed to support the subscription, resolve billing questions, and meet legal and accounting obligations.

09Security

On-device information is protected by the security of your iOS device itself — the same protections (passcode, encryption at rest, Face ID/Touch ID) that protect the rest of your phone. We encourage keeping your device's own lock screen and software up to date, since that's the primary safeguard for anything stored in the App.

No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but because so little information ever leaves your device, EBP‑SLP's exposure is limited almost entirely to the anonymous subscription data described in Section 4, which is handled by Apple and RevenueCat's own security programs.

10Children's privacy

EBP‑SLP is a professional clinical reference tool intended for licensed and student speech-language pathologists. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. Given the App has no account system, this risk is minimal by design.

11Your choices

Because we don't hold a profile of personal information about you, there generally isn't data on our side to access, correct, or export on request. In practice, your controls are:

  • To see or remove your on-device data — it's already on your device; deleting the App removes all of it.
  • To manage or cancel your subscription — use your Apple ID's subscription settings, the same as any other App Store subscription.
  • To turn off notifications — change your preference anytime from the Profile tab, or disable them at the iOS level in Settings.

If you have a question about anything in this policy or how a specific feature handles information, reach out using Section 13 and we'll do our best to answer directly.

12Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as EBP‑SLP's features change. When we do, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we'll aim to flag it inside the App as well. We'd encourage checking back here occasionally, particularly after an app update.

13Contact

Questions about this policy or how EBP‑SLP handles information are welcome.
