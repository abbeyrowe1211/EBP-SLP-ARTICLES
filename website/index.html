Evidence-based tools for speech-language pathology.

Rowe to Recovery LLC is a small healthcare technology company founded by a licensed medical speech-language pathologist. We build clinical software that puts peer-reviewed research directly into the hands of practicing clinicians.

Our product

EBP‑SLP

A mobile app for medical SLPs that curates clinically-applicable, peer-reviewed research by ASHA practice area — every article graded by its level of evidence. Clinicians select the research that fits their patient, and EBP‑SLP generates a structured session plan built directly from those findings, ready to run and to document with trial-by-trial data collection.

Evidence grading used throughout the app

1a · Systematic reviews 1b · RCTs 2 · Cohort studies 3 · Case studies
