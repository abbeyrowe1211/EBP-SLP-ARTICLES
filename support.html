Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save an article?

Open any article and tap the Save button in the top-right corner. Saved articles are available in the Saved tab.

How do I generate a session plan?

From any article detail page, tap Generate session plan from this article at the bottom of the screen.

What does the bell icon show?

The bell highlights recently added articles so you never miss new evidence-based content.

How is my data used?

Please see our Privacy Policy for full details on data collection and use.
